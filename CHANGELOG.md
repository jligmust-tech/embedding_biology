## [Unreleased]

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-12

### Changed
- **Strict dimension notation.** Adopted one rule book-wide: `d` is the
  feature/embedding dimension; `k` is a count (k-mer length, k-NN size, top-`k`,
  matrix rank) and, inside an explicit reduction, the reduced dimension (`d → k`,
  `k ≤ d`). Applied to Chapter 10 (knowledge-graph embeddings `ℝ^k → ℝ^d`) and to
  Appendix A's notation table and overload list; Chapters 2 and 3 were already
  consistent.
- Citation sweep executed over all 213 entries (Crossref + DataCite):
  **0 metadata corrections required** (`verification/citation-sweep.md`).

### Added
- Two computed figures generated by `tools/make_figures.py`:
  a linear-vs-nonlinear projection comparison (Chapter 2, `@fig-dim-reduction`)
  and an ROC-vs-precision–recall imbalance figure (Chapter 11, `@fig-roc-pr`).
- Integrity checker `tools/check_book.py` (citation keys, cross-reference
  labels, global label uniqueness, placeholders) and a `Book checks` CI workflow
  that also runs a render smoke test.
- `LICENSE` (text/figures: CC BY 4.0; code: MIT) and this `CHANGELOG.md`.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-12

### Added
- The full book: 14 chapters (Foundations, Biological Modalities, Practice) and
  Appendices A (mathematical background), B (tools/datasets), C (glossary),
  written by parallel subagents against `outline.qmd`.
- A math-enhancement pass and an **independent** math-verification pass:
  153 numbered equations checked, 23 corrected, 16 cross-cutting issues
  reconciled (`verification/math-review.md` and per-group logs).
- `verification/` logs: math review, notation consistency, citation review,
  bibliography conflicts.
- `tools/normalize_refs.py` (globally unique equation labels) and
  `tools/consolidate_bib.py` (213-entry consolidated `references.bib`).

### Fixed
- Assorted mathematical corrections found during review (e.g. a PCA
  argmax/argmin error, the mismatch-kernel definition, a scaling-law
  attribution) and a wrong arXiv identifier in the bibliography.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-12

### Added
- Initial Quarto book scaffold: `_quarto.yml`, preface, custom theme,
  `outline.qmd` (master outline with per-section requirements), chapter
  placeholders, and the GitHub Pages deployment workflow.
